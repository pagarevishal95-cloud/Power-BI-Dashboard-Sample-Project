# Power-BI-Dashboard-Sample-Project - Decathlon Retail Performance Analysis

**Decathlon Retail Performance Dashboard**

**Overview**

This project analyzes retail sales performance, profitability dynamics, and product performance metrics for Decathlon using Power BI.
The dashboard compares Year-To-Date (YTD) performance against the Prior Year-To-Date (PYTD) period to give regional managers and retail leads actionable insights into growth drivers, regional demand, and margin health.

**Dataset**
 
•	Key Fields: Net Sales, Gross Profit, Gross Margin %, Units Sold, Orders, Average Order Value (AOV), State, Sport Type, Product Tier, Store Name, Payment Method, Month, Region, Brand Label.
 
 **KPIs (YTD vs. PYTD Comparison)**
 
•	Net Sales YTD: ₹ 55.26M (+24.3% YoY vs. PYTD ₹ 44.45M)

•	Net Gross Profit YTD: ₹ 21.72M (+25.3% YoY vs. PYTD ₹ 17.33M)

•	Gross Margin YTD: 39.3% (+0.3 percentage points YoY vs. PYTD 39.0%)

•	Net Units Sold YTD: 46K (+23.1% YoY vs. PYTD 38K)

•	Net Orders YTD: 24K (+23.0% YoY vs. PYTD 20K)

•	Net AOV YTD (Average Order Value): ₹ 2.82K (+24.3% YoY vs. PYTD ₹ 2.27K)

 **Dashboard Charts & Visuals**
 
**Retail Performance (Sales & Profitability Overview)**

•	KPI Scorecard Matrix: Six performance blocks showing absolute metrics, previous year benchmarks, and year-over-year percentage trends.

•	Line Chart (Monthly Net Sales): Current Year (CY) vs. Previous Year (PY) trend analysis tracked from January through September.

•	Horizontal Bar Chart (Net Sales YTD by Sport): Breakdown of sales volume by specific sporting activities (led by Hiking at ₹12.1M).

•	Scatter Plot (Product Type Performance): Cross-analysis comparing Net Sales YTD (X-axis) against Gross Margin YTD (Y-axis) to isolate high-revenue vs. high-margin items.

•	Donut Chart (Net Sales YTD by Region): Geographic market share distribution split among South, West, North, East, and Central zones.

•	Horizontal Bar Chart (Net Sales YTD by Brand Label): Revenue distribution across Decathlon passion brands (led by Quechua at ₹12.08M).

•	Slicer / Filter Panel: Left-hand navigation pane allowing real-time data drilling by State, Sport, Product Tier, Store Name, and Payment Method alongside a "Reset Filters" action button.

 **Key Structural Insights (From Snapshot Data)**
 
•	Primary Sales Driver: Hiking stands out as the dominant sports category, generating ₹12.1M in sales—nearly double any other single sport.

•	Flagship Brand Value: Quechua acts as the anchor brand for this period, accounting for a major portion (₹12.08M) of the total revenue structure.

•	Geographic Stronghold: The South region contributes the largest single market slice, driving 31.69% (₹17.5M) of overall Net Sales.

 **Tools**
 
•	Power BI Desktop

•	DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Used for advanced Time Intelligence measures to calculate YTD metrics, PYTD baselines, and YoY % growth shifts.
